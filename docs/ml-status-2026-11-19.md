Open issues:
- Membership anchors: editions without an official count (NeurIPS 2016-2019, ICLR 2017-2020, ICML 2016-2020, 2022-2023) pass as `anchored: false`; every count that exists reconciles within 1%.
- NeurIPS 2021 is missing its 174 Datasets & Benchmarks papers (their listing uses `round1`/`round2` track labels the listing parser does not read).
- Four papers have an empty author list in the official NeurIPS listing itself: 2016 `5d616dd3...` (its PDF is a different paper, 'Launch and Iterate'), 2017 `861dc9bd...`, 2018 `959a557f...`, 2019 `08040837...`. They are a documented exception (`data/refresh/ml-authorless-papers.jsonl`): they earn no credit and no longer block `byline_integrity`.
- Stored extracts: the 12 editions re-judged above carry v8 extracts (ICLR 2021 carries v6); the other 16 passing editions keep their judged extracts from older extractor versions, because re-extracting changes content-hashed batches and forces re-judging. Re-extract and re-judge before any from-scratch rebuild.
- Extractor v8 closed the last two failures (ICML 2022 at 99.5%, NeurIPS 2016 at 100.0%): a footnote key printed on its own line under the text it labels is put back before that text, and the first line of a footnote entry that starts a little past the margin under a raised key is read; several names over one centred stack of lines (one line reaching under every name) share that single affiliation; legend columns continue under the entry that starts at the same column; `⇧` is a mark. Against the ~52,200 image-judged slots: +263 matches, 4 lost (judged omissions of a printed city). A broader centred-block rule from v7 was rejected for wrong credit; this one needs a single stack of one-column lines and no keyed list.
- Extractor v6 (2026-11-20): `?` (pdftotext's rendering of an unmapped symbol such as ⋆) is a mark; each group of names reads the legend printed under it; "work was done" footnotes are noise; a page-bottom footnote never supplies a shared affiliation to unmarked authors. Against about 52,700 image-judged slots across all editions: +203 matching slots, 2 lost (both judge omissions the page text shows). Only ICLR 2021 has been re-extracted. For it, verdicts were carried over verbatim where the task was byte-identical, and the 5 changed tasks were re-judged. Batch 13 was judged by Sonnet because Haiku twice confused ⋆ with ∗ on ChipNet (`xCxXwTzx4L1`).
- Membership (user, 2026-11-20): as for every other venue, official counts prove each source on recent editions; older editions from the same source need no count of their own and are reported `anchored: false`. Secondary counts such as PaperCopilot are not used (they read the same listings we harvest). After this change 17 of 29 editions pass every gate: the 13 above plus ICLR 2017, ICLR 2020, NeurIPS 2018 and NeurIPS 2019. ICML 2025 still fails membership (3,330 vs 3,260: 73 position papers to filter); 11 editions fail only extractor accuracy (ICLR 2018-2019, ICML 2016-2020, 2022-2023, NeurIPS 2016-2017; ICML 2025 also).
- Extractor v7 (2026-11-21): PMLR footer tails ("Proceedings of the NN th ...", "Cor-"/"Correspon-") are stripped; words broken across lines are rejoined; a footnote start whose text runs into citations, a numbered list item, or an "N Introduction" heading is body text; a lone left-column key and keys over a run-together PMLR footnote line split it at the word under the key; key ranges ("1-3") expand; bracketed keys ("[1]") are read in the author block only; glued single-digit keys ("Grant12") split when the legend has no two-digit key; "work ... carried out/performed" notes are noise; `⇤` is a mark; email-group fragments are dropped. Tried and rejected for causing wrong credit: widening a column block to a shared centred block; splitting semicolon lists under one key; splitting at key columns in the author-block legend.
