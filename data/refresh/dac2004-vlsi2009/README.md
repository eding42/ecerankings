# DAC 2004 and VLSI Technology 2009

DAC 2004 had no data and VLSI Technology 2009 held 18 records, most of them
unrelated or Japanese-language entries. Prepared 2026-11-26.

## DAC 2004

`dac-2004-contents.csv` lists the numbered items parsed from the SIGDA
compendium contents page, with their Crossref DOIs and page lengths. The edition
keeps the 163 papers of four or more pages in regular sessions. A UC Irvine CECS
newsletter reports 163 accepted technical papers. Items under "Special Session"
headings and panels are left out; one such paper, under a misspelled heading, is
not in the CSV. Crossref also files a few 20-33 page records under the same DOI
prefix; they are not in the contents list and are left out.

Authors and affiliation text come from Crossref. OpenAlex institutions were
added where both author lists had the same length and a compatible name in every
position (160 of 163 papers).

## VLSI Technology 2009

`vlsi-technology-2009-program.csv` holds the 82 papers on the symposium's
technical program, which states that 82 papers were selected. Items marked
Invited and the welcome are left out. Authors and affiliations are as printed;
affiliation markers were matched to author names by script. Universities resolve
through the affiliation map, plus exact institutions for a few unambiguous
strings the map lacks. Strings naming only a university system stay unresolved.
